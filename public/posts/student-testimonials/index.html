Student Testimonials

May 18, 2026

One of the reasons why I wanted to advocate for this topic is because many of my peers have had issues with the current dining experience at Seattle University. So, I decided to interview a few of my peers on what issues they have with the dining experience, and what they want to change.

Interview Questions:

  • How would you rate your SU dining experience 1-10?
    • K: 5
    • C: 4
    • M: 6
    • Mo: 4
    • E: 5
  • Do you have any specific issues with dining?
    • K:The quality in the essence sometimes is overcooked to a terrifying degree. Food poising, the fruit and vegetables are expired and don’t taste right. I feel like I’ve gotten rice before, and somehow despite it being rice they’ve messed it up. Overall disappointed and I won’t eat at certain places if they serve certain things.
    • C: Some of the staff seem like they are drunk and give very small portions.
    • M: I don’t like how rude the sandwich lady is, I stopped going because she was mean to me.
  • How do you feel about the price of items for dining?
    • K: OMG, the price is so expensive for the quality you get. It’s not good quality food, it’s not cooked half the time, or its crunchy, or there’s a bone.
    • C: Stuff is overpriced, and it can be cheaper to eat off campus, Carmelo’s as an example. The entrees are 3 dollars more expensive than they should be.
    • M: A lot of stuff is expensive, and it keeps going up, and it’s stupid because we already pay a shit ton for being here like laundry. The quantity is equivalent to giving us scraps.
    • Mo: Horribly overpriced, especially for the small portions they give us.
    • E: Too expensive for the portion sizes.
  • Do you feel like there is enough variety for dining?
    • K: I feel like there is variety when things are open, but half the time things are closed because they have weird hours. Fusion closes at 5 for example, right at dinner time. They also serve the same thing, over and over and over again.
    • C: The hours that the variety is good are not good enough. There is also a dipropionate amount of Indian food.
    • M: It depends, sometimes, but the variety isn’t as good as they advertise. Stuff is closed on the weekend and it’s dumb.
    • Mo: Not really, it’s either the overpriced food that actually tastes good, but we don’t get a lot of it unless we pay for double portions, or stuff out of the cave or Redhot Rudy’s, but that makes you sick.
    • E: Not enough variety; I see them rotate the same few items throughout each location, or they repeat the same item.
  • Have you felt like you have proper access to a kitchen to cook?
    • K:Oh no, God no! I do not have the pots and pans, and when I ask for them, they do not have any. How am I supposed to boil water?
    • C: Not Seattle U’s fault, not in my case.
    • M: Not really, no. It’s there, but it’s very out of reach.
    • Mo: No, not really, we have a kitchen to cook in but no supplies.
    • E: No, I either have to pay for my own pots/pans which I can’t afford.
  • How often do you eat on campus compared to cooking/eating out?
    • K:So, I only get food from campus, because I don’t like spending actual money.
    • C: I eat about 75% of my meals on campus, the rest I go out for.
    • M: 85% of meals is eaten on campus.
    • Mo: I eat on campus pretty frequently, or buy stuff from the cave to portion out, 95% I eat on campus.
    • E: Pretty much all on campus. I don’t have money to go out.
  • How do you feel about meal plans, and that they are required for 1st and 2nd years?
    • K: I don’t like the meal plan; I’d rather get good food off campus with a set aside amount of money.
    • C: I wish we were able to spend the money on chain restaurants like Chipotle.
    • M: I wish we could take the money required for the meal plan and eat off campus.
    • Mo: I don’t really like that, even if we did have access to proper cookware, we still would only be able to work with food from the cave.
    • E: They shouldn’t be required, or if they are, they should be a lower amount.
