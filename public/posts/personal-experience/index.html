Personal Experience

May 18, 2026

As someone with food allergies, my dining experience at Seattle University has been severely limited because of them. There is only one place on campus I can consistently eat at, which is Thrive Without Nine. But there are multiple problems with Thrive Without Nine. There have been consistent problems with it, mainly the pricing and the portion sizes. The price per calorie at Thrive is much higher when compared to other campus spots such as Fusion or the Bistro, and on average they give less calories. The small portion sizes/lack of calories have directly caused me to lose weight this year, and I’ve dropped approximately 10lbs since starting in the fall. Whenever I go to eat on campus, it feels like my hands are tied and I am forced to eat at Thrive. Whenever I try to eat anywhere else I run the risk of getting cross contaminated, which has serious dehabilitating effects on my health and academic performance. I have already repeated gotten sick, causing me to miss class and even quizzes. This has caused me to personally lose faith in Seattle University’s dining, because it is hard not to feel scammed every meal, and hard to trust that the food provided is actually safe for people with sensitive allergies like me.
