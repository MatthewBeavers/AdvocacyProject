My name is Matthew Beavers, and I am a sophomore at Seattle University. I was inspired to create this project because I have Celiac disease and am also allergic to cow milk. This project was designed for Seattle University students. I was also inspired to create this project because of the book Revolutions a Very Short Introduction by Goldstone. I read this book for my UCOR 3400 class, and while reading it I noticed that there were two elements of revolution from the book that could be applied to this project. The first is fiscal strains, given that we are college students and during a time when the economy due to the current United States administration is suffering. (Goldstone, 2023) The second is “increasingly widespread popular anger at injustice.” (Goldstone, 2023) Many feel like they are being scammed with the dining experience at Seattle University, and the more time I spend at this university, the more I notice how upset people are with dining.
