So, how do we address change? There are a couple of different options that Seattle University could take. One study from a California school found that students express “a desire for practical financial and food literacy “life skills” training” (Watson, et al. 2016) Given the Jesuit nature of our university, with the value of cura personalis, Seattle University could offer a training on food literacy during orientation. This could include budgeting, where to find food resources on campus, perhaps an expediated application for the food pantry at the training as well. Like I say in the research section, there could also be free ready-to-eat meals at The Cave.
